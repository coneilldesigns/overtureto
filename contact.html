FAQ

What is Overture Studios?

A creative space in Toronto for DJs, producers, artists and creatives. We offer professional DJ and music production studios, memberships, courses, events and artist development.

Where are you located?

163 Sterling Rd, Toronto, ON M6R 2B2. Open in Maps.

Do I need to be a professional DJ or producer to book?

Not at all. Overture is open to beginners, experienced artists, and anyone who wants a professional space to practise, create or build their skills.

Can I book a studio without a membership?

Yes. You can book any studio by the hour without becoming a member.

What equipment is available?

Professional DJ and production gear, including CDJ-3000s, DJM-V10, DJM-900NXS2, Xone:96, Technics SL-1200s, production equipment, monitors, samplers and more. Equipment varies by room; see Studios for the full list.

Which room should I book?

It depends on what you're doing:

  • Nova: DJ practice and getting comfortable on professional club equipment.
  • Hybrid: DJing, vinyl and music production.
  • Vortex: a bigger DJ setup and content creation.
  • Production Suite: focused music production.
How many people can I bring?

Each room has its own capacity: Nova up to 6, Hybrid up to 6, Production Suite up to 5, and Vortex Practice up to 5. The Vortex Events space holds up to 20, with larger events available by arrangement.

Can I connect my own laptop?

Yes, depending on the room and setup. Bring your own connection gear.

Do you offer DJ lessons?

Yes. Our DJ courses help you build technique, mixing, track selection and confidence behind the decks.

Do you offer music production lessons?

Yes. The music production course covers Ableton Live, sound design, arrangement, mixing and finishing complete tracks, and includes studio time and track evaluation.

What are Overture memberships?

Memberships give you recurring studio access at a lower effective hourly cost. Current options are Core, Pulse, B2B and Grid, each with different monthly hours and benefits. Compare plans.

Can I book a studio for an event?

Yes. The Vortex Events space is designed for DJ events and holds up to 20 people. For larger events or special setups, contact us directly.

Can I record my DJ set?

Yes. You can do it yourself, or if you need a specific recording setup, contact us before booking.

Is there parking?

There's parking around Sterling Road, but availability varies. Leave some extra time for parking and arrival.
