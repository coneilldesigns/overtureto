## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Multi-page, mirroring the current site's routes (Home, Studios, Plans, Learn to DJ, Learn Music Production, Contact). Room booking and plan/course checkout link out to the studio's existing Optix flows (overture.optixapp.com); contact, free-class and newsletter forms open a pre-filled email to axel.e@overtureto.com. Policy pages still link to the Wix site.

## Users

- Toronto DJs and producers, from complete beginners to working artists, looking for professional club and production gear by the hour.
- People wanting structured 1-on-1 tuition: DJ Foundations (5 classes), DJ Development (16 classes), Ableton Music Production (16 classes).
- Creative duos and small teams (B2B membership) and event organisers renting the Vortex Events room (up to 20 people).

## Product Purpose

Overture Studios is a creative space at 163 Sterling Rd, Toronto (Junction Triangle). It rents DJ and production rooms by the hour, sells monthly studio memberships, teaches DJ and production courses, hosts events, and operates as a label ("a record label, a creative studio, and an evolving platform for underground expression"). Success is bookings, memberships, and free-class signups.

## Positioning

Owner-run underground studio with club-standard gear (CDJ-3000, DJM-V10, Xone:96, Technics SL-1200 MK7) in four distinct rooms, plus a path from student to showcase/open decks to label release consideration. "Not just booking a room — joining a movement."

## Operating Context

- Memberships: Core $98 (6h), Pulse $160 (12h), B2B $216 (16h, split), Grid $270 (20h). Monthly, valid until cancelled.
- Contact: axel.e@overtureto.com, 437-990-6146, Instagram @overture.studioss.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Overture / Overture Studios. Taglines: "Where creativity meets possibility", "Move to create.", "Join the movement", "Level up your sound."
- Logo: circular swirl mark (white on black), files in assets/img.
- User-pinned aesthetic for the web rebuild: dark techno, heavily animated, modern, fast, responsive.
- Brand colour (user, 2026-11-17): black with white text, raw and "hesh" (heavy, gritty, metal/skate edge). No purple or violet: it is not their brand. Red is kept only for booking actions.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real studio photography of every room, events, community lounge, and gear (downloaded from the current site).
- One real testimonial (Cristiana, DJ and producer). No others exist; do not fabricate reviews, stats, or alumni.
- FAQ content from the Contact page.

## Product Principles

1. The gear and rooms are the proof: show them, name them, spec them.
2. Every page leads to an action: book a room, pick a plan, or claim the free class.
3. Underground, community-first voice; never corporate.
4. Beginners are welcome; never gatekeep.
