# Overture site: all marketing pages

Scope: index, studios, plans, learn-dj, learn-production, contact. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Toronto DJs and producers, beginner to working artist. Actions: book a room (Optix), buy a membership (Optix signup), claim a free class.
Proof: real room photos, the room-by-room gear lists, membership prices and hours, one real testimonial, FAQ.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, fast; booking and checkout link out to overture.optixapp.com; forms write a pre-filled email to axel.e@overtureto.com.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a dark room at the door of a warehouse party, and the visitor holds the UV torch. Everything is stamped in stamp ink and turns into the real thing only where the light falls. It refuses the stock club template (full-bleed video, a centred headline and a grid of cards).
OWN-WORLD: Pure black ground #000000; stamp ink #f2f2f2 for all printed matter; hot white #ffffff where the light lands; a single red #ff3b3b for live/booking state. Big Shoulders Display Black, set as rubber-stamped type with an SVG ink-bleed filter; Archivo for running text, expanded for labels; JetBrains Mono only for gear specs, hours and prices. Photos ship as two-tone Bayer-dithered ink prints with full colour underneath. Stamp-edged rules, perforated ticket stubs, and hour bars whose length is the exact number of hours.
STORY: A beginner or working DJ understands that this is a pro club-gear studio on Sterling Rd with four named rooms, believes it by seeing the rooms and gear under the light, and books a room, picks a plan, or claims the free class.
FIRST VIEWPORT (home, revised by the user on 2026-11-17): nothing but the header and the Overture logo, centred and large (about 62% of the shorter side, 74% on phones), extruded in chrome and spinning and floating in a drifting starfield (Three.js, js/space.js); the visitor can drag it to spin. The stamped OVERTURE over the Vortex plate, the line, BOOK A ROOM / See the plans and the four-room ticket strip move to the second screen (#intro). Inner pages keep the stamped header over a plate.
FORM: Blacklight Door Stamp, position 6 of my grounded list. Seed key e37f1c22. Raises: two-tone dither discipline (one-bit desktop); length = duration hour bars (labanotation); one gesture drives every reveal (miura).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
